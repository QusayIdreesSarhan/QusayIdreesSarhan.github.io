About Me

Dr. Qusay Idrees Sarhan received his B.Sc. degree in Software Engineering from the University of Mosul, Iraq, in 2007, his M.Tech. degree in Software Engineering (scholarship) from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, India, in 2011, and his PhD degree in Software Engineering (scholarship) from the University of Szeged, Hungary in 2023. Currently, Dr. Qusay is an assistant professor at the University of Duhok, Iraq. His research interests include Software Engineering (static and dynamic program analysis, software testing, software quality, etc.), Internet of Things (data analysis, cybersecurity, embedded systems, IoT applications, etc.), and Artificial Intelligence (datasets, algorithms, LLMs, AI/ML applications, etc.). Dr. Qusay has over 40 publications in national and international publication venues and is regularly invited to serve as a reviewer for specialized conferences and journals.

Research Interests

My research interests span the following areas:

Software Engineering

Static and dynamic code analysis, software testing, software debugging, software quality, and software fault localization.

Internet of Things

IoT data analysis, IoT resource-constrained devices, IoT embedded systems, IoT security, and IoT applications.

Cybersecurity

Security aspects related to software, IoT, embedded systems, and connected systems.

Generative AI & LLMs

Datasets, algorithms, Large Language Models, and AI/ML applications.
