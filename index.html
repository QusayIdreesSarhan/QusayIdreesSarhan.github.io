About Me

Dr. Qusay Idrees Sarhan is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Duhok, Iraq. He received his B.Sc. degree in Software Engineering from the University of Mosul in 2007, his M.Tech. degree in Software Engineering from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University in 2011, and his PhD degree in Software Engineering from the University of Szeged, Hungary, in 2023.

His research interests include Software Engineering, Internet of Things (IoT), Cybersecurity, Generative AI, and Large Language Models (LLMs).
